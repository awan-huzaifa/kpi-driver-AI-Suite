Cross-App View

Platform-Wide Metrics

Revenue per active company

Broken down by which apps they use. Tells you which apps drive real wallet spend vs which are vanity features.

Multi-app adoption

% of companies using 3+ apps — your stickiness and expansion revenue signal

App unlock rate

Of companies on the platform, how many unlock each app? Low = onboarding problem.

Wallet burn rate per company

Which apps consume the most credits? Concentration = where value lives

Ingest failure rate

Webhook errors, unmatched agents — operational health. Silent failures = invisible churn.

Time from invite → first value event

Activation speed. Long = churn risk before they ever see value.

Cost per company per month

Anthropic + Stripe fees vs revenue. Margin health.

Revenue per active company broken by app is the roadmap metric. If Skip Tracing drives 40% of wallet spend but Training Coach drives 5%, that's your prioritization answer.
