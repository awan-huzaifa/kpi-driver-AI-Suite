Sequencing

What to Measure First

1

AI Accuracy

Validate first

Manager override rate + score agreement. Without this everything downstream is built on a lie.

2

Score vs Close Rate

Prove ROI

If you can prove this, you have a sales argument for every new client. Enables product-led growth.

3

Adoption Metrics

Scale usage

Events per week, multi-app adoption, activation speed. Push engagement once quality is proven.

4

Cost Per Event

Optimize margins

Optimize once you know what "good" looks like at scale. Premature optimization wastes engineering time.

Don't skip to adoption if accuracy isn't proven. Every engagement metric built on a bad AI is measuring churn in slow motion.
