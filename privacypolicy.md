## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how **KPI DRIVER, LLC** ("KPI DRIVER," "we," "us," "our") collects, uses, discloses and protects information in connection with our websites and the KPI DRIVER platform (together, the "Platform").

The Platform is a customer relationship management and business automation system for real estate investing businesses, combined with a suite of artificial intelligence applications:

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| **CRM** | Contact and pipeline management, calling and texting, email, appointment booking, workflow automation, funnels and reporting |
| **AI Dashboard** | Answers questions and generates reports across the data in your account |
| **AI Agent** | Answers inbound calls and messages, converses with leads, qualifies them and books appointments |
| **AI Sales Coach** | Scores and coaches on live sales calls |
| **AI Training Coach** | Scores practice calls conducted against AI voice agents |
| **AI Deal Underwriter** | Produces property valuation and offer analysis |
| **Note Taker** | Generates call summaries and structured notes |
| **Data Enrichment** | Populates and enriches contact fields |
| **Skip Tracing** | Performs parcel, Do-Not-Call and contact data lookups |

**Who this policy addresses.** The Platform is sold to businesses, for business use. It is not offered to consumers for personal, family or household purposes. Three groups are covered:

- **Visitors to our websites** — anyone browsing kpidriver.com or kpidriver.ai.
- **Our customers and their users** — the businesses that subscribe and the individuals who log in. As to these people we are a **controller** (a "business" under California law).
- **Individuals inside a customer's account** — sales leads, property owners, contacts, and anyone the AI Agent speaks with. As to these people we are a **processor** (a "service provider"). **The business using the Platform controls that information and is responsible for it. Direct your requests to them.** Section 16.6 explains how we help.

## 7. Artificial Intelligence

### 7.1 How AI is used

Generative AI processing is performed by third-party large language model providers under commercial agreements. **We do not build or train our own models.** We build the rubrics, prompts, agent configurations, evaluation logic and calculation engines around third-party models.

Content transmitted for AI processing includes: call transcripts, AI Agent conversation content, records retrieved by the AI Dashboard, property addresses and characteristics, comparable property data, and contact field data.

Skip Tracing does not use AI. It performs a direct database lookup.

### 7.2 Your data is not used to train AI models

**We do not use Customer Data to train, fine-tune or develop artificial intelligence models.** Our large language model providers are contractually prohibited from doing so.

**One exception applies to the AI Agent.** The provider delivering that feature reserves broader rights over conversation content under its own terms. See Section 11.2.

There is no opt-out because there is nothing to opt out of. If this position ever changes, we will notify customers in advance and will not apply the change to data already submitted.

Our AI providers may retain submitted content for a limited period for abuse monitoring and legal compliance under their own terms. That retention is outside our control and is not affected by deletion from our systems.

### 7.3 Human review

**By us.** We do not routinely read recordings, transcripts, AI Agent conversations, AI outputs or lookup results. Authorized personnel access Customer Data only to diagnose a fault a customer has reported, investigate a security incident or suspected abuse, or comply with a legal obligation. Such access is restricted by role and logged.

**By our providers.** Our AI providers may conduct automated and, in limited circumstances, human review for safety and abuse purposes under their own terms.

### 7.4 Accuracy and limitations

AI outputs — dashboard answers and reports, AI Agent conversation content, call scores, coaching recommendations, summaries, property assessments, valuation ranges, offer recommendations and enriched field values — are **statistical estimates generated by a language model. They can be incomplete, inaccurate, or confidently wrong.**

- **Dashboard answers may be wrong.** The Dashboard interprets a question, retrieves records and generates an answer. It may misinterpret the question, retrieve the wrong records, miscount, or state a figure with confidence the underlying data does not support. **Verify any number before relying on it.**
- **AI Agent conversations may contain errors.** The Agent may misunderstand someone, state something inaccurate, qualify a lead incorrectly, or book an inappropriate appointment.
- **Call scores** reflect a rubric applied to a text transcript. They cannot capture tone, context or relationship history.
- **Underwriting output is not an appraisal.** After-repair values, repair estimates, maximum offer figures and profit projections are automated estimates from third-party data. They are not prepared by a licensed appraiser, do not comply with the Uniform Standards of Professional Appraisal Practice, and must not be represented to anyone as an appraisal, broker price opinion or professional valuation.
- **Enriched data may be wrong.** Automatically populated fields are inferences.
- **Nothing produced by the Platform is legal, financial, tax, investment, real estate, employment or professional advice.**

**Users are responsible for reviewing AI output before acting on it.**

### 7.5 Automated evaluation of individuals

**Scoring of representatives.** The Sales Coach and Training Coach features assign numerical scores from 0 to 100, with penalties, bonuses and category ratings, to identifiable employees.

> **This is an evaluation of a person's job performance produced by an automated system.** If a customer uses these scores to make or support decisions about hiring, promotion, compensation, discipline or termination, that is an AI-assisted employment decision, which may trigger obligations under the Colorado AI Act, California's automated decision-making technology regulations, New York City Local Law 144 and comparable statutes — including duties to notify the employee, conduct bias testing, provide an explanation and offer human review.
>
> **We have not performed a bias audit of our scoring rubrics, and we do not represent that scores are free from bias or suitable as the basis for any employment decision.** Our terms require that scores never be the sole basis for a consequential employment decision and that a human review every such decision.

**Lead qualification.** The AI Agent asks qualification questions and reaches a qualification outcome about an individual. **The Platform must not be used to determine any individual's eligibility for credit, insurance, housing, employment or any other benefit governed by the Fair Credit Reporting Act.**

## 8. Automated Calling and Messaging

### 8.1 What the AI Agent does

The AI Agent converses with people by telephone using an artificial voice, and by text message. It asks qualification questions, answers questions about our customer's business, and books appointments.

**It operates primarily by responding rather than initiating:**

- **Outside a customer's configured business hours, it answers inbound calls only.** It does not place outbound calls.
- **It replies to inbound text messages**, and continues a conversation for as long as the recipient continues it.
- **During business hours, it may follow up with a lead where no human has responded.**

### 8.2 If you received a call or text from an AI Agent

**The business that contacted you decided to contact you and supplied your telephone number.** KPI DRIVER provides the software. That business is responsible for having the legal right to contact you.

**To stop being contacted:** reply **STOP** to any text message, or tell the AI Agent during a call that you do not want to be contacted. Either is recorded as an opt-out and honored. You may also contact the business directly.

**If contact continues after you opted out**, or if you believe you were contacted unlawfully, email **info@kpidriver.com**. We will forward your request to the business and assist in resolving it, and we will record your request so it can be honored across the Platform.

**You are entitled to know you are speaking with an AI.** Our terms require customers to configure the Agent to disclose that it is an artificial intelligence system, and prohibit configuring it to claim to be human or to deny being an AI if asked. If that did not happen, tell us.

### 8.3 Consent

Under a February 2024 Federal Communications Commission declaratory ruling, an AI-generated voice is an "artificial voice" under the Telephone Consumer Protection Act. Consent requirements attach to **initiating** a call — answering a call someone placed, or replying to a message someone sent, is not initiating contact.

Where the AI Agent initiates outbound contact, **our customer is solely responsible for having and documenting the required consent**, and for observing calling hour restrictions. We do not obtain consent and cannot verify that our customer has it.

## 9. Call Recording

**Calls made and received through the Platform are recorded and transcribed, including calls answered by the AI Agent.**

**Recording law applies regardless of who placed the call.** Florida is an all-party consent state under Fla. Stat. § 934.03, meaning recording generally requires the consent of every party. Numerous other states impose comparable requirements, and the applicable rule may depend on where each participant is located.

**Our customer is solely responsible** for obtaining all legally required consents, for delivering any required notice at the start of a call, and for configuring the Platform's recording settings consistently with its obligations. **KPI DRIVER does not obtain consent and does not verify that it was obtained.**

If you were on a recorded call and were not told, that is a matter between you and the business that recorded it. Contact them directly; we will assist them in responding to you as described in Section 16.6.

## 10. Skip Tracing and Contact Data Lookups

This feature returns personal information about individuals — including property owners — who have not interacted with our customer and are generally unaware a lookup has occurred. It is the most sensitive processing on the Platform.

**What we do.** On our customer's instruction, we query a third-party contact data provider using an address, parcel identifier or telephone number, and return and store the results.

**What our customer must do.** Have a legitimate business purpose for each lookup; ensure it is permitted under applicable law; **not** use results for any purpose governed by the Fair Credit Reporting Act; comply with all telemarketing law before contacting any number returned; and not use results for stalking, harassment or any unlawful purpose.

**Do-Not-Call and litigator flags** are informational signals from a third-party database. **We do not warrant their accuracy, currency or completeness, and they are not a substitute for a customer's own scrubbing.**

**Accuracy.** This data is compiled from public and commercial sources and is frequently outdated or wrong. It may associate a telephone number or address with the wrong person.

If you pause your subscription, your core records are preserved so you can resume where you left off — contacts, conversations, opportunities, tasks, users, funnel and automation configurations, calendars, uploaded media and files, products, orders and transactions.

**Some things are not preserved.** Telephone numbers and A2P messaging registration are deleted by the underlying telephony provider a short period after a pause begins — currently 14 days. Marketplace and add-on subscriptions, including the AI Agent, are cancelled — and **AI Agent conversation history, recordings and configuration may not survive a pause or be recoverable on reactivation.** Published funnels, automations and booking links revert to draft. See Section 6.12 of our Terms and Conditions.

**Storage-intensive data in the AI Suite is an exception.** Derived media and generated files we hold in the AI Suite layer carry ongoing storage cost. While an account is paused or inactive, **we may delete this data unless you arrange with us to pay for continued storage.** We will notify you at the email address on your account before we do, and give you a reasonable opportunity to export it or to elect paid retention.

> **Deletion is permanent and we cannot restore it. Export anything you may need before pausing.** This matters most for **call recordings**, which may be your only evidence that a person consented to be recorded or contacted.

If an account has no login activity and no usage for **twelve consecutive months**, we may treat it as dormant. We will notify you at the email address on your account and give you at least **30 days'** written notice and an opportunity to export before deleting anything further.

### 13.4 Termination

On termination you have **30 days** to export. After that we retain your data in an inactive state so you can reactivate, unless you ask us to delete it — in which case Section 13.2 applies. Section 13.3 continues to apply to terminated accounts.

### 13.5 Records we keep regardless

**Consent, opt-out and Do-Not-Call records are retained indefinitely**, including after everything else is deleted. Removing them would destroy the evidence that a person asked not to be contacted, which protects both you and them.

Financial transaction records are retained for seven years as an audit trail.

### 13.6 Backups

Information may persist in encrypted backups after deletion from live systems, and is removed when those backups are overwritten. We do not restore deleted data except in a disaster recovery event, and we re-apply pending deletions to restored data.

### 13.7 Deactivated users

Where an individual user is deactivated rather than deleted, their profile record remains associated with their organization so historical records remain intelligible. Their access is revoked immediately. Full deletion is available on request from the organization.

### 13.8 Aggregated data

Aggregated and de-identified statistics that cannot reasonably be linked to an individual may be retained indefinitely.

## 14. Security

We maintain administrative, technical and physical safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration and destruction, including:

- **Least-privilege internal access**, granted only where operationally required
- **Audit logging** of authentication events, administrative access and feature usage
- **Written agreements incorporating data protection terms** with every service provider

**Certifications.** KPI DRIVER does not currently hold SOC 2, ISO 27001 or comparable independent certification.

**No system is perfectly secure.** We cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your credentials, deactivating departing users promptly, and configuring access roles appropriately.

## 18. Cookies and Tracking

### 18.1 What cookies are

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website. Similar technologies include local storage, session storage and pixels. This section uses "cookies" to refer to all of them.

### 18.2 What we use

**Strictly necessary cookies.** Required for the Platform to function and cannot be switched off. These maintain your authenticated session, keep you signed in, provide request forgery protection and security, handle load balancing and request routing, and support fraud prevention on payment pages. Set by us and by our authentication, hosting and payment providers. Duration ranges from session-only to 30 days.

**Functional cookies.** These remember your interface preferences and record your cookie consent choice. Set by us. Duration up to 12 months.

**Marketing website cookies.** Our public websites are built on a third-party website platform and include an embedded appointment booking widget. These set cookies necessary for page delivery, form functionality and booking widget operation. Duration ranges from session-only to 12 months.

### 18.3 What we do not use

**We do not use advertising cookies, retargeting pixels, or cross-site tracking technologies. We do not participate in advertising networks.**

### 18.4 Managing preferences

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies through their privacy settings. **Blocking strictly necessary cookies will prevent you from signing in.** Functional cookies can be disabled with limited effect.

### 18.5 Global Privacy Control

We honor the **Global Privacy Control (GPC)** signal. If your browser or extension transmits GPC, we treat it as a valid request to opt out of any sale or sharing of personal information and of targeted advertising. Because we do not sell or share personal information or engage in targeted advertising, GPC does not change how we handle your data — but we recognize and record the signal.
