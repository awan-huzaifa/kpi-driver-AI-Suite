## 9. Calling, Messaging and Recording

**This Section allocates the most significant regulatory risk in this agreement. Read it carefully.**

### 9.1 How the AI Agent operates

The AI Agent converses using an artificial voice for calls and automated messaging for texts. **It operates primarily by responding rather than initiating:** outside your configured business hours it answers inbound calls only and does not place outbound calls; it replies to inbound text messages and continues a conversation while the recipient continues it; and during business hours it may follow up with a lead where no human has responded.

Answering a call someone placed to you, or replying to a message someone sent you, is generally not a "telephone solicitation." **Initiating outbound contact is, and carries the obligations in Sections 9.3 and 9.4.**

### 9.2 Recording — applies to everything

**All calls made and received through the Platform are recorded and transcribed, including calls answered by the AI Agent. Recording law applies regardless of who placed the call.**

**Florida is an all-party consent state under Fla. Stat. § 934.03**, as are numerous other states, and the applicable rule may be determined by the location of any participant. Violations carry criminal penalties and a private right of action.

You represent, warrant and covenant that you:

(a) **configure the AI Agent to deliver a clear recording notice at the very start of every call** it answers or places, before any substantive conversation;
(b) provide equivalent notice on human-handled calls;
(c) obtain consent from **every party** to every recorded call;
(d) determine the correct rule for each call based on where every participant is located;
(e) stop recording if a participant declines or withdraws consent;
(f) maintain records evidencing the notice given, and produce them on our reasonable request; and
(g) never submit to the Platform a recording or transcript obtained unlawfully.

**Test the recording notice before launch and after every configuration change.**

### 9.3 AI disclosure

You must ensure that every person who interacts with the AI Agent is told, **at the beginning of the interaction**, that they are speaking with an artificial intelligence system. You must not configure the Agent to claim to be human, to use a real employee's identity in a way implying a person is speaking, or to deny being an AI if asked.

Deception rules apply regardless of who initiated the call. Misrepresenting an AI system as human carries exposure under the Florida Deceptive and Unfair Trade Practices Act, the FTC Act and a growing set of state AI disclosure statutes.

**AI Agent voice.** The AI Agent speaks using synthetic voices made available natively on the Platform and supplied by third-party voice technology providers. **The Platform does not offer voice cloning, and you must not attempt to introduce, upload or use a cloned, replicated or imitated voice** — whether of an employee, a public figure or any other person.

Your use of any voice made available to you is subject to the terms of the provider that supplied it. You must not use any voice in a manner that misrepresents the identity of the speaker, imitates a specific real person, or infringes any person's rights of publicity, likeness or voice.

### 9.4 Consent, hours and Do-Not-Call — outbound contact

Where you or the AI Agent **initiate** outbound contact, you represent, warrant and covenant that you:

(a) have obtained and documented **all consents legally required** before contact. An AI-generated voice is an "artificial voice" under the Telephone Consumer Protection Act following the Federal Communications Commission's February 2024 declaratory ruling. Marketing content generally requires prior express **written** consent; responsive or informational content generally requires prior express consent;
(b) maintain **evidence of the inbound inquiry** relied on wherever you treat a contact as responsive, with date and time;
(c) configure the AI Agent so outbound contact occurs **only within lawful calling and messaging windows** for each recipient's location. Federal law prohibits telephone solicitations before 8:00 a.m. or after 9:00 p.m. **in the recipient's local time**. **Florida law is stricter: solicitations end at 8:00 p.m., are prohibited on Sundays, and are limited to three calls per number per day on the same subject.** Other states impose their own restrictions. Apply the strictest rule applicable to each recipient;
(d) scrub against the **National Do-Not-Call Registry**, applicable state registries and your own internal list before outbound solicitation, and re-scrub at least every 31 days;
(e) do not contact numbers obtained from purchased lists, lead aggregators or third-party sources unless you have verified that valid, transferable consent naming your business exists;
(f) **do not load contact data lookup results into any outbound campaign** without independently obtained consent — a number from a lookup carries none;
(g) transmit accurate caller identification and never spoof or misrepresent it;
(h) identify the business on whose behalf each contact is made; and
(i) comply with all applicable carrier and campaign registration requirements, including 10DLC or equivalent registration for application-to-person messaging.

### 9.5 Opt-outs

**A person may revoke consent at any time, by any reasonable method** — replying STOP or similar to a text, saying so verbally to the AI Agent or a human, emailing, or telling any employee. You must not require a specific word, format or channel.

You must **configure the AI Agent to recognize and honor an opt-out request immediately, in any phrasing**, including verbal opt-outs mid-conversation, and to stop continuing a conversation the person has clearly ended. Revocation must be honored promptly and must suppress the number **across every channel and every campaign**.

**Test opt-out handling before launch and periodically after.**
